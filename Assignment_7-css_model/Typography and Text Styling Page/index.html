Mastering Web Typography

Typography is perhaps the single most critical element of effective web design. It determines not only the aesthetics but also the readability and usability of your entire site. Without careful attention to detail—from font selection to line height—even the most beautiful layout will fail to engage the reader. We explore techniques to bring text to life.

Font Families and Weight

Choosing the right font family sets the entire tone. Serif fonts like **Georgia** often convey formality, while sans-serif fonts such as **Arial** are popular for screen readability. This text uses a combination to demonstrate the contrast. The use of strong and emphasis tags must be consistent across the document for hierarchy.

Spacing and Alignment

Line height, or leading, is the vertical space between lines of text, crucial for avoiding dense blocks of text. Letter spacing, or tracking, adjusts the horizontal space between characters. Proper alignment ensures a clean left edge for the eye to follow, enhancing the overall professional look of the article.
